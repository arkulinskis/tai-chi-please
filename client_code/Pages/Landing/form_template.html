Don't forget the lymph: a second circulation, with no pump of its own

Your lymph system carries fluid, waste and immune cells back toward the heart. It moves with breathing, movement and gentle pressure, which makes a slow morning practice its natural friend. Every morning flow includes the classic self-massage sequence:

  1. Deep belly breathing
  2. The hollows above the collarbones
  3. The sides of the neck and around the ears
  4. The armpits, then the arms
  5. Slow circles on the belly
  6. The hip creases, then the thighs and the backs of the knees

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Press Begin and practise. Your sessions are kept on this device; create a free account whenever you want your journal, streak and path on every device, and they come with you.

I have never done tai chi. Is this for me?

Yes. Start with the 7-minute morning flow. Every move is shown slowly, from any angle, with each step explained, and you learn one new move only when the last one feels familiar.

What is lymphatic self-massage? Is it safe?

Light, slow strokes that stretch the skin toward the lymph nodes, helping fluid move back toward the heart. For most people it is gentle and safe. If you have an infection, a blood clot, heart failure, kidney disease, cancer or lymphedema, ask your doctor first. It is a wellness practice, not a treatment.

How much space do I need?

About two steps in every direction. A bedroom, a balcony, a quiet corner of a park.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions.

Who can see my practice?

Only you. Others see how many people practised this morning, and your first name only if you choose to join the morning circle.
